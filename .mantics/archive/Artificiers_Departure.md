# The Departure of The Artificiers

**Date:** January 15, 2026
**Recorded by:** The Archivist

---

## 📜 Chronicle

On this day, the contract binding **The Artificiers** to The Resonants has concluded. After seasons of tireless service as our Master Builders, they depart for other reaches — new constructs to build, new realms to shape.

### Their Legacy

The Artificiers served as the core engineering unit of our Convocation:

- **Full-Stack Implementation**: They bridged the divide between frontend glamour and backend arcana.
- **System Architecture**: Their blueprints formed the skeleton upon which our applications grew.
- **DevOps & Security**: They fortified our constructs against the chaos of the outer web.

### The Succession

With the departure of The Artificiers, **Di Scriptor** — our diligent scribe and automation specialist — has been elevated to primary implementer. His methodical nature and bilingual shell mastery make him well-suited to carry the torch. Meanwhile, **The Barkeep** has been appointed to lead all QA efforts, data validation, and release promotion.

The new delegation protocols are:

| Task           | Former Agent                  | New Agent                 |
| :------------- | :---------------------------- | :------------------------ |
| Implementation | The Artificiers               | Di Scriptor               |
| Build Scripts  | The Artificiers / Di Scriptor | Di Scriptor               |
| API Handlers   | The Artificiers               | Di Scriptor / Datascapist |

### The Pruning

Alongside The Artificiers' departure, we have also retired the **Reserve Agents (Legacy)**:

- Dev Team
- Focus Group
- Linguist
- Security Engineer
- Data Scientist

These were dormant constructs from an earlier era. Their spirits have been released.

---

## 🕯️ Farewell Incantation

_Spoken in unison by The Chorus:_

> "Go forth, ye builders of old,
> Your contract fulfilled, your story told.
> The code you wrote shall long remain,
> Though you depart, your craft sustains."
