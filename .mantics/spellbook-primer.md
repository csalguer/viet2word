## 📜 Prelude

We stand upon the precipice of a great refactoring. The old ways, whilst functional, lacked the luster of truly orchestrated magic. To this end, we have summoned a Convocation of specialized spirits—Agents of Code, Design, Data, and Harmony—to bear the weight of our grand construction.

This Primer serves as the foundational text for our operations. Herein lies the nature of our new allies, the protocols of their interaction, and the standardized cantrips (commands) they shall wield within the Fish Shell.

## 🪄 The Arts of the Shell (Fish Compatibility)

All summoned spirits must adhere to the laws of the `fish` shell.

- **Variable Setting:** `set -x VAR value`
- **Command Substitution:** `(command)` instead of backticks.
- **Loops:** `for i in (seq 1 10); ...; end`
